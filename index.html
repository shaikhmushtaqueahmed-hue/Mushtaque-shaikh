KPIs that drive FMCG sales

A short guide to the measures used to run a distribution territory, and how they were delivered at Tapal Tea.

Target achievement

Sales against the monthly target, tracked for every distributor, brand and pack (SKU).

ATL / BTL activity

Above-the-line (advertising) and below-the-line (in-store and trade activation) tasks executed on the ground.

Unique productivity

The number of different outlets that bought in a period. More outlets buying means healthier, wider coverage.

Growth rate

Sales growth against the same period last year, for the territory or the whole zone.

Brand-wise and pack-wise

Making sure every brand and pack size hits its own number, not only the overall total.

Distributor health

Stock, coverage and service levels of each distributor, managed so that every one reaches target.
